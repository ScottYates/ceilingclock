Printing the parts

Nine files in hardware/stl/, each saved the way it should sit on the bed: load it and don't rotate it. The pictures below are rendered from those files.

Settings for every part

  • Material: PLA or PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle.
  • Walls 3, top and bottom 4 layers, infill 15–20 %.
  • Colour: matte black for the barrel, display holder, lens mount and focus ring. Light bouncing around inside lighter plastic washes out the time on the ceiling. The rest can be any colour you like.
  • Layer height: 0.2 mm, except the two threaded parts (lens mount, focus ring) at 0.12–0.16 mm so the thread turns smoothly.
  • Supports: only where a part below says so.

After printing, screw the focus ring into the lens mount before fitting the lens: it should turn by hand with a slight drag. If it's stiff, run it in and out a few times.

Base base.stl

Size170 × 135 × 72 mm
ColourDark
Layer height0.2 mm
SupportsNone

The biggest print (170 × 135 × 72 mm), floor flat on the bed. The front display window, the USB-C slot and the side button hole are small bridges your slicer handles without help. Stick four 6 mm rubber feet under the corners afterwards: they lift the floor so the speaker can breathe. Use base_056.stl instead if your front display is a 0.56" module.

Lid lid.stl

Size170 × 135 × 4 mm
ColourDark
Layer height0.2 mm
SupportsNone

Prints upside down, top face on the bed, so the top comes out smooth. The pin ramps underneath face up.

Barrel barrel.stl

Size68 × 68 × 113 mm
ColourMatte black
Layer height0.2 mm
SupportsNone

Stands on its open end. Tall and narrow: add a 5 mm brim so it can't be knocked over. The mirror ramp inside is a 45° slope and prints as it is.

Display holder disp_holder.stl

Size65 × 64 × 16 mm
ColourMatte black
Layer height0.2 mm
SupportsNone

Lies on the face that points at the mirror. The slot for the display opens out of its edge.

End cap end_cap.stl

Size60 × 60 × 37 mm
ColourDark
Layer height0.2 mm
SupportsInside only

Stands on its open collar, pin tabs up. The flat ceiling inside is a 46 mm bridge: turn on supports set to touching the build plate only; they stand inside the cap and pull out through the collar. Nothing touches the outside.

Lens mount lens_mount.stl

Size87 × 62 × 54 mm
ColourMatte black
Layer height0.12–0.16 mm
SupportsUnder the two tabs only

Upside down, round top on the bed. Paint supports (or use support enforcers) under the two pin tabs that stick out of the sides, and leave automatic supports off: they would fill the thread inside.

Focus ring, 61in sleeve_61in.stl

Size61 × 61 × 23 mm
ColourMatte black
Layer height0.12–0.16 mm
SupportsNone

Grip down. Pick this one (or sleeve_44in.stl) by the distance from the lens to your ceiling: 61in for about 50–75 in, 44in for about 36–55 in. The number is engraved on its top face. Turn on elephant's-foot compensation (about 0.2 mm): the lens pocket is on the bed side.

Lens cap, 61in lens_cap_61in.stl

Size52 × 52 × 14 mm
ColourAny
Layer height0.2 mm
SupportsNone

Ring on the bed, tube up. Use the cap with the same number as the focus ring: its tube is cut to the depth of that ring's lens pocket.

Battery tray batt_tray.stl

Size74 × 66 × 10 mm
ColourAny
Layer height0.2 mm
SupportsNone

Only if you fit the battery, and only for the 5000 mAh pouch cell: a bigger pack goes in the bay without it. Prints flat as it is, 74 × 66 × 10 mm. The pocket is 67.5 × 56.6 mm, 1 mm larger than a 65.5 × 54.6 mm cell all round so it isn't squeezed: for a cell of another size, change pouch in enclosure.scad and export again.

Twist-lock pins pins.stl

Size54 × 30 × 12 mm
ColourAny (PETG is a little tougher)
Layer height0.16 mm
SupportsNone

Wing heads flat on the bed. One file prints the whole set: short pins for the lid corners, long ones for the end cap and the lens mount. Print a spare set; they are small.
