Buttons on the clock

Alarm 1 and Alarm 2Press: switch that alarm on (the front display shows "1 on", then its time, and the button lights for a moment) or off ("1 oF", two blinks). While an alarm rings, either one stops it. Holding longer does the same as a press.
Snooze (top)While ringing: press to snooze ("SnoZ", then when it rings again); hold 2 s to stop ("StoP"). Otherwise: shows which alarms are on ("AL12" both, "AL1-" only alarm 1, "AL--" none), lights their buttons, and shows the time on the ceiling for 15 s.
Side buttonPress: front display on or off ("d on" / "d oF"). Hold 3 s: ceiling projection on or off ("P on" / "P oF"). Keep holding to 10 s: forget the Wi-Fi and restart in setup mode ("rSEt").
MessagesChanges made on this page show on the front display too, so the clock always says what just happened.
