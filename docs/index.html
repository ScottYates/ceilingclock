A ceiling projection alarm clock that sets itself

An ESP32-S3 keeps time from internet time servers and projects it onto the ceiling. A battery-backed clock chip keeps it running through outages. Alarms, sounds, sunrise light and the displays are all set from your phone.

    Always on time

    Syncs with NTP every hour and handles daylight saving automatically. A DS3231 keeps time when Wi-Fi or power drops, so alarms still fire.

    Alarms your way

    Up to 8 alarms with their own days, sound, fade-in, snooze, sunrise lead and auto-stop. "Skip next" handles days off.

    Dark when you want

    The front display and the projector can each be always on, fully off, night-only or day-only, with separate day and night brightness.

    Try the clock's web page live → The real firmware runs in your browser: set an alarm, fast-forward time, press the buttons.
