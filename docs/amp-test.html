Testing the amplifier

No sound from the speaker? Three checks, in order, find where it stops: power to the amp, the three signals from the ESP32, and the amp's output. Written for the FNIRSI 2C53T, which is a multimeter and an oscilloscope in one, but any meter and scope work the same way.

What's in the 2C53T box, and what you need here

  • Oscilloscope probes (P6100, ×1/×10): one or two, depending on the bundle. Each has a BNC plug, a ×1/×10 switch on the handle, a hook tip and a ground clip. You need one.
  • Multimeter test leads, red and black. You need both.
  • BNC-to-crocodile-clip cable: that one is for the signal generator output. Leave it in the case.

The scope inputs are marked CH1 and CH2, and the signal generator output sits beside them; use CH1. The multimeter has its own banana sockets: COM, 10A, mA and the volts/ohms socket. To change mode, hold the home key to get the function selection page, then pick Oscilloscope or Multimeter.

Before you start

  1. Wire the amp as in the amplifier step, speaker connected, 5 V adapter and USB plugged in.
  2. On the clock's page, under Sounds, play a sound to have something to measure. A long, steady sound is easiest to read (the built-in beep is mostly silence).
  3. Every check measures from the amp's GND. The amp's pins are covered by the jumpers underneath, so probe the silver solder joints on top of the board.

Check 1: power

  1. Plug the black lead into COM and the red lead into the volts/ohms socket (not 10A or mA). Switch the 2C53T to its multimeter.
  2. The meter picks AC or DC volts by itself, but its automatic mode doesn't work below about 0.7 V, and SD sits right around there. If SD reads blank or 0, step the meter to DC volts by hand: short presses of the function key cycle through AC/DC, diode/continuity and so on.
  3. Black tip on the GND joint. Red tip on VIN, then on SD.

Check 2: the signals from the ESP32

  1. Push the probe's BNC plug into CH1 and twist it to lock. Set the switch on the probe's handle to ×10, and set CH1's probe setting on the scope to ×10 too: the manual says both must be on the same setting, or the voltages read 10 times wrong.
  2. Push a spare jumper into the bottom − rail and fix the ground clip on its metal end. Leave it there.
  3. Switch the 2C53T to the oscilloscope. Pull the hook sleeve back and touch the sharp tip to the LRC joint, then press AUTO once. Repeat for BCLK and DIN.
  4. Using both channels? Put both probes' ground clips on the same GND point. The manual forbids connecting the two ground clips to different points.

All three look right but there's still no sound? Go to check 3.

Check 3: the amp's output

  1. Keep the ground clip on the − rail.
  2. Touch only the probe tip to the + speaker screw.
⚠️ Never clip the ground lead to either speaker screw. The amp drives both speaker wires, and the scope's ground is joined to the circuit's ground through USB, so the clip would short an output.
